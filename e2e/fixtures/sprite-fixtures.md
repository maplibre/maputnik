Sprite fixtures authored for this test suite and distributed under the repository MIT license. The 96×32 base atlas contains a small red rectangle, a green square, an analytic circle distance field (alpha=0.75+signedDistance/8), and a blue 2× square. The 192×64 `@2x` atlas uses doubled positions and dimensions for the base sprites and keeps the blue square at its original 2× resolution. The alternate catalog serves the blue square through the base atlas.
